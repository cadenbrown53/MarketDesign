# Market Design - ECN 591

**Authors:** Caden Brown & Eli Riggins

## Course Overview
This repository contains coursework and assignments for **ECN 591: Market Design** taught by Professor Tomas Larroucau at Arizona State University (Spring 2026).

Market design is the area of economics where economists analyze and improve the way markets work. The course explores how economists can help design better market mechanisms by combining theory, computational modeling, experiments, and understanding of existing institutions.

## Repository Purpose
This repository serves as a centralized workspace for course assignments and GitHub work, representing a collaboration between Caden Brown and Eli Riggins, both students in ECN 591. The repository contains:
- Course assignments and problem sets
- Presentations and analysis of market design literature
- Code and materials related to course projects
- Documentation of learning progress throughout the semester

## Course Topics
The course covers key areas in market design including:
- Matching markets and stable allocations
- School choice mechanisms
- Medical residency matching
- Auction design
- Market failures and their diagnosis
- Evaluating and comparing market designs
